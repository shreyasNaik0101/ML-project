# Data sources

Contains public sector information from the UK Department for Transport licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/). The prepared datasets in this repository are derived from these files.

- The 2025 collision and vehicle CSVs and the field codebook (`stats19-guide.xlsx`) are from [DfT Road Safety Open Data](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/road-safety-open-data).
- `dft_traffic_counts_aadf.zip`, `count_points.zip`, and `mrdb-2025.zip` are from [DfT Road Traffic Statistics](https://roadtraffic.dft.gov.uk/downloads). The model comparison uses the 2025 AADF records and 2025 Major Roads Database geometry.
